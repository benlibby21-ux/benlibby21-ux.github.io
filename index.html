Midnight Coffee

Concept & pitch · Co-leading creative direction
Camera operation · Collaborative editing

Watch on YouTube ↗

Different worlds. One shared ritual.

I proposed using match cuts between different people and industries enjoying Midnight Coffee to show the range of customers the brand serves. The idea connected those different settings through one shared experience: drinking their coffee.

I helped pitch the concept, co-led the shoot as a camera operator and creative director, and contributed to editing the majority of the film.

02 / Television advertisement

RAGBRAI

Collaborative concept · Voiceover scripting
Camera operation · Collaborative editing

Watch on YouTube ↗

Bring the ride to the screen.

RAGBRAI approached our team about creating a television advertisement. We pitched a concept centered on what it feels like to ride RAGBRAI, bringing together iconic moments riders can experience along the way.

I helped script the voiceover, filmed content, and contributed to the edit. This was part of my first year producing content for RAGBRAI, followed by a second year focused on social content.
